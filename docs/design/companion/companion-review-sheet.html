6 · Specification

  • Grid: 24 × 32 (up from 16 × 16, which can't hold this level of detail). Head 13 rows (about 40%) over a slim body, legs, and sneakers.
  • Sizes: 2× (48 × 64 px) while walking the page; 1× (24 × 32 px) in the sidebar, which fits the space of the 36 px "CB" tile. The teleport hides the size change. At 64 px tall the route uses the open space between sections, not the 32 px gaps between cards.
  • Frames: 12 unique — idle 2, walk down 4 (2 unique steps + stand), walk sideways 4, teleport 4; arrival reuses the teleport reversed.
  • Timing: walk down 6 fps, sideways 8 fps, teleport ~110 ms/frame, blink about every 3 s. Stepped, never smoothed.
  • Likeness: casual photo — hair swept up and to the right, glasses, smile, white mock-neck sweater. Skin and hair tones from the formal portrait. Jeans and sneakers invented (the photo stops at the waist); sneaker soles use the site's vermilion.
  • Craft: near-black outline, three tones per material lit from the top left, layered hair highlights, half-rim glasses with a white glint, sweater folds, shaded jeans. Facing down, the stepping foot lifts while the other stays planted; sideways, the far leg is darker and the near arm swings.
  • Day / Night: identical art; Night adds a light outline (#9fb3d6) only while the character is visible.

Palette. Skin and hair from the formal portrait; glasses and sweater shade from the casual photo; teeth, soles, beam, and sparks use site colours.
