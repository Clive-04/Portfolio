6 · Specification

  • Grid: 16 × 16 (the site's icon grid), shown at 2× = 32 px on the route and in the sidebar — the same size as the "CB" tile, so the swap moves nothing and the sprite fits the 32 px gap above cards.
  • Frames: 12 unique — idle 2, walk down 4 (2 unique steps + stand), walk sideways 4, teleport 4; arrival reuses the teleport frames reversed.
  • Timing: walk down 6 fps, sideways 8 fps, teleport ~110 ms/frame, blink about every 3 s. Stepped, never smoothed.
  • Likeness: from the casual photo — dark hair swept up and to the right with fringe pieces, thin round glasses, big smile, white mock-neck sweater. Skin and hair tones come from the formal portrait (the casual photo's overcast light turned them grey-blue). Dark jeans and shoes are assumed; the photo is cropped at the waist.
  • Style: selective outlines (dark beside hair and jeans, warm brown beside skin, cool grey beside the white sweater), three tones per material, light from the top left.
  • Day / Night: the art is identical. Night adds a 1-pixel light outline (#9fb3d6) around the silhouette, only while the character is visible (not around teleport light).

Palette. Skin and hair from the formal portrait; glasses and sweater shade from the casual photo; teeth, beam, and sparks use site colours.
