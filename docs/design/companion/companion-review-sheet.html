5 · Specification

  • Grid: 16 × 16, the site's icon grid. Rendered at 2× (32 px) on the route and in the sidebar, the same size as the current "CB" tile, so the swap causes no layout shift.
  • Frames: 10 unique — idle 2, walk 4, teleport 4 (arrival reuses the teleport frames reversed). The walk is mirrored with a transform for leftward steps.
  • Timing: walk 8 fps (125 ms/frame), teleport ~110 ms/frame, blink once every ~3 s. Stepped, never smoothed.
  • Likeness cues from the photo: swept black hair with a curtain fringe, warm tan skin, dark eyes, thin moustache and smile, white collar, black suit, dark tie with light stripes.
  • Night: a light outline (#9fb3d6) and slightly lifted jacket and hair, so the dark suit doesn't vanish on navy. Day keeps the site's navy outline.
  • Teleport colours: the site's own cyan beam and yellow sparks.

Palette (Day / Night). Skin, hair, jacket, eyes, and tie were sampled from the photo, then fitted to the site palette.
